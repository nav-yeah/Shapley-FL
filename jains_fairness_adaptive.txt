Interpretation: within the rounds where Shapley weighting is actually active, J
near 1.0 means the contribution-derived weights stay spread across the 30 HAR
clients rather than collapsing onto a few dominant ones — i.e. the method
reweights by contribution without starving anyone out of the aggregate.
